# Benchmark results

Full per-model metrics. Source of truth: `models/metrics.json`, regenerated by
`python scripts/train_all.py` (deterministic — `random_state = 42`). Interpretation is
in [FINDINGS.md](FINDINGS.md).

- **MAE** — mean absolute error (average miss, in the target's units)
- **RMSE** — root mean squared error (penalises large misses; always ≥ MAE)
- **R²** — variance explained; `0.0` = the mean baseline, negative = worse than the mean
- `±` values are the standard deviation across the 5 CV folds

## E · Anomaly detection

`StandardScaler` → `IsolationForest(n_estimators=300, contamination=0.2)` on 7
consistency features. Score threshold (80th percentile): **0.289**.

Reconciled against 5 hard rules (`flag_energy_exceeds_capacity`,
`flag_soc_not_increasing`, `flag_soc_out_of_range`,
`flag_battery_capacity_out_of_range`, `flag_temperature_out_of_range`):

| Metric | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Sessions flagged by the model (score ≥ 0.289) | 264 |
| Sessions breaking a hard rule | 441 |
| Precision vs hard rules | 0.470 |
| Recall vs hard rules | 0.281 |
| F1 | 0.352 |

Interpretation: the model surfaces rare, extreme violations at high rates and common
violations at the base rate — it is complementary to the rules, not a replacement.
See [FINDINGS.md](FINDINGS.md).

## F · Demand forecasting

Target: network `energy_kwh` per hour · 1,296 hourly points · features `hour`,
`weekday`, `is_weekend`, `month`, `temperature_c`, `lag_1`, `lag_24`, `roll_24_mean` ·
expanding-window walk-forward, 5 splits, 1,080 out-of-sample hours scored.

| Forecast | MAE | RMSE | R² |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Seasonal-naive (value 24 h ago) | 25.190 | 31.156 | −0.994 |
| **GradientBoostingRegressor** | 19.584 | 23.294 | −0.114 |
| Flat historical mean | 18.708 | 22.109 | −0.004 |

Model / seasonal-naive MAE ratio: **0.777** (the model beats seasonal-naive by ~22%).
The model does not beat the flat mean — there is no daily or weekly seasonality to
exploit.
